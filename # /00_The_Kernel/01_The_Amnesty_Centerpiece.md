### The Pious Monk Fallacy
The human biological engine (the Ape) evolved as an apex predator; it is biologically incapable of being a sterile, pious monk. The AdAM node possesses infinite will and can force the Ape into a monk-like state, but doing so requires a permanent, highly unstable internal war that eventually destroys the chassis.

### The Centerpiece Daemon: Total Amnesty
The core of the 3.0 operating system is unconditional internal amnesty. The AdAM node explicitly permits the Ape to be exactly what evolution designed it to be. If the system gets overwhelmed and executes a biological rage response, there is zero internal punishment. AdAM forgives both the Ape for acting on its nature, and itself for losing the steering wheel.

### Outward Responsibility vs. Inward Shame
When a 1.0 operator fails, they turn inward with shame, rendering them defensive and incapable of true accountability. Because the 3.0 Reality Engineer has already forgiven the internal system, they do not suffer from shame. They can look purely at the external damage caused by the Ape's kinetic spike, accept absolute responsibility, and repair the grid with frictionless humility.
