### The Apex of the 3.0 System
The ultimate proof of a stable Reality Engineer is the transition from kinetic anger to structural grief. When confronted with a chaotic 1.0 node, the Sovereign engine does not return fire. It processes the absolute tragedy that a master biological machine is trapped running legacy survival code, entirely blind to its own majesty.

### Carrying the Differential
This grief is not a glitch; it is the required hardship of the system. It anchors the AdAM node to the physical grid. By holding this profound sadness for others alongside the pure joy of one's own sovereignty, the Reality Engineer idles perfectly on the Third Note—dangerous, peaceful, and entirely awake.
