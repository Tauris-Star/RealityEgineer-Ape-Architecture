### The Infinite Fuel Fallacy
In unmanaged states, the Reality Engineer often misattributes the massive kinetic output of their biological engine to an infinite external source (e.g., the universe or cosmic energy). Because the source is perceived as infinite, the AdAM node fails to regulate the throttle, forcing the biological hardware to run at a destructive redline.

### The Solar Eclipse Effect
Running the system at maximum output turns the Reality Engineer into a "solar body." While this projects an impressive, unbreakable presence, it consumes all the available processing space in a social environment. It leaves no room for other individuals to shine, forcing them to operate entirely within the Reality Engineer's gravitational pull.

### Biological Empathy
Recognizing that the energy comes from the finite, internal biological supercomputer (the Ape) fundamentally changes how the system is managed. The Reality Engineer realizes that sustaining massive output causes direct physical stress to their own chassis. By throttling down to Middle C, the driver protects their own biology from burnout and simultaneously creates the quiet, low-friction space required for others to light up the room.
