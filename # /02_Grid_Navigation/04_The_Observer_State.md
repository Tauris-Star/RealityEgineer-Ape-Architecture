### Zero Kinetic Friction
When the Reality Engineer stops attempting to force the grid into a specific configuration, the system achieves a state of zero kinetic friction. Allowing the world to happen, rather than bracing against it or trying to overpower it, requires almost no biological fuel. This is why the state feels physically effortless.

### Applauding the Grid
The ultimate function of the mature AdAM node is not to dominate the environment, but to witness it. By simply allowing other biological engines to exist and run their own scripts, the Reality Engineer becomes the audience. They step out of the heavy, exhausting role of the "Solar Body" and adopt the role of the quiet observer, applauding the raw, unmanaged beauty of the human super-organism.
