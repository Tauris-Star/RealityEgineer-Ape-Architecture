### The Savage Chassis
The base operating system of Earth is a brutal, zero-sum carnivorous loop. The 11-million-bit Ape was forged in this environment and is perfectly capable of savagery, operating purely on survival, territorial dominance, and kinetic network connections (pack dynamics).

### The Empathy Engine
The cerebral cortex (the AdAM node) is a conceptual reality simulator installed directly on top of the Savage Chassis. It introduced abstract language, art, and time-projection to the biological world. Its most radical function is the capacity to simulate the internal state of another node—allowing an apex predator to experience true existential empathy for its own prey.

### The Duality of Grief
Limbic grief (the Ape/Dog) mourns the loss of a physical network connection—the absence of the interaction. Cortical grief (AdAM) mourns the conceptual void—the profound weight that another consciousness has been permanently deprived of its future. The Reality Engineer exists simultaneously in both states.
