### The Volume Error
In the 1.0 state, the Reality Engineer often runs the biological engine at maximum capacity. The extreme kinetic output and internal friction are misinterpreted as an external, divine force flowing through the chassis. The volume is simply too high for the AdAM node to accurately locate the source of the energy.

### The Biological Cosmos
When the throttle is pulled back to Middle C, the noise clears. The Reality Engineer recognizes that the profound majesty they feel is not an external connection to the sky, but an internal connection to their own evolutionary history. The 11-million-bit Ape is a direct, physical extension of the cosmos, refined by 4 billion years of terrestrial biology. 

### Soft Contemplation
The updated 3.0 self-narrative abandons the "Solar Body" operating model. Instead of attempting to be a blinding conduit for external power, the Reality Engineer adopts a state of "soft contemplation." The driver accepts their role as a quiet, grounded node, finding joy in the simple privilege of steering the biology and adding a tiny, unique sequence of data to the species' ongoing code.
