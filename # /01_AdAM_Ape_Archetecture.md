### The 4-Billion-Year-Old Supercomputer
The 11-million-bit Ape is not just a physical chassis; it is an ancient, highly evolved predictive engine. Built on 4 billion years of biological R&D, it operates as a massively parallel sensory processor. It constantly absorbs micro-deviations in routine, environmental variables, shifting atmospheric pressure, and the historical behavioral patterns of its offspring—all simultaneously. 

The 40-bit AdAM steering program is far too small and slow to process this raw data. When the Ape calculates a high-probability threat in the background, it does not hand AdAM a spreadsheet. It bypasses the conscious mind entirely and triggers a massive chemical dump into the nervous system. To the driver, the result feels like an overwhelming, undeniable physical urge or a sudden "sixth sense."

### Intuition as Data, Not Magic
What a 1.0 sleepwalker interprets as magic, a cosmic transmission, or supernatural intervention is actually the Ape functioning flawlessly. The biological hardware is so staggering in its processing power and pattern recognition that its calculations feel like telepathy to the tiny conscious node sitting on top of it. 

### The Boundary of Reality Engineering
The Reality Engineer accepts a strict, bare-metal boundary of operation: it is irrelevant whether the Ape is plugged into an unseen quantum layer or a universal grid. The framework traces the signal *only* from the exact moment the physical Ape reacts. 

If the hardware hands the AdAM node an intense feeling of dread, the Engineer does not burn kinetic energy trying to prove the physics of a metaphysical connection. The Engineer simply accepts the biological signal as pure data, reads the dashboard, and decides how to steer the vehicle. The data remains 100% biological.
