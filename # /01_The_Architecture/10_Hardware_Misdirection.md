### The Chemical Translation Error
The biological hardware (Ape) frequently translates physical discomfort—pain, exhaustion, hunger, or poor posture—into generalized chemical anxiety. The conscious steering program (AdAM) detects these stress chemicals and instinctively generates negative or panicky thoughts to explain the physical sensation. 

### The Diagnostic Pivot
When the system is caught in a loop of upsetting thoughts, the Reality Engineer must pause the logic script and run a strict hardware diagnostic. 
1. **Sensory Override:** Open the audio/visual apertures to starve the internal panic script of processing space. 
2. **Hardware Check:** Assess the physical chassis. Is the body in pain? Is it exhausted? Is it out of alignment?
3. **External Pivot:** Do not attempt to logic your way out of a physical problem. Apply external physical solutions (posture correction, medication, hydration) to stop the chemical distress signal at its source.
