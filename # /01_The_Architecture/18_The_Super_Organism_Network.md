### Chemical Belonging
The "sense of belonging" in a super-organism is a physical, chemical tether. Through continuous fluid exchange (trophallaxis), individual nodes pass hormones and scent markers, creating a shared biological data stream. This is the hardware equivalent of human familial bonding—a profound, hardwired connection to the greater network.

### The Illusion of the Monarchy
The "queen" does not issue commands or steer the colony. She is solely the reproductive hardware of the hive. The actual steering of the super-organism is a decentralized consensus generated by the millions of worker nodes reading the environment and adjusting their kinetic output accordingly. 

### The System Override
When the network experiences extreme stress, the worker nodes will prioritize the survival of the super-organism over the life of any individual component—including the queen. If her output fails, the decentralized consensus will cleanly abandon or dismantle her to conserve the network's remaining fuel.
