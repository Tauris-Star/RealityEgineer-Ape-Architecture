### The Conscious Wave (Cross-Brain Communication)
The conscious steering program (AdAM) is not a single point; it is a highly compartmentalized network spread across the neocortex (vision, language, logic, memory). The sensation of "I" is an emergent property—a standing wave created only when these separate nodes synchronize their electrical frequencies and share data across the brain simultaneously. 

### The Screen Saver (REM Sleep)
During REM sleep, the underlying biological hardware (Ape) remains highly active, firing off kinetic energy, memories, and sensory data. AdAM's visual and narrative nodes remain powered on to process this data, attempting to weave the random signals into a cohesive story (dreams). However, the specific prefrontal node responsible for strict logic and reality-testing is muted. AdAM sits in the passenger seat, watching the Ape hallucinate without the ability to steer.

### System Maintenance (Delta Sleep)
During deep, slow-wave sleep, the biological hardware executes critical physical maintenance (tissue repair, memory consolidation, toxin flushing). To achieve this, the Ape drops massive, slow electrical pulses across the cortex. These Delta waves physically block the high-frequency cross-brain communication between AdAM's compartments. 

When the compartments can no longer synchronize, the network effect collapses. The "I" completely powers down. The Reality Engineer ceases to exist, proving that the driver is a temporary, synchronized wave of data, entirely dependent on the hardware's electrical state.
