### The Evolutionary Echo
Mundane grid friction (like traffic) triggers deep survival code. A vehicle following closely registers to the Ape as an unseen predator. A slow vehicle in front registers as a blocked escape route. The resulting panic and annoyance are not software glitches; they are flawless biological executions of ancient telemetry.

### The Sterile AdAM Error
When a "Sterile AdAM" attempts to reject these natural emotions—feeling frustrated at the fact that it is feeling frustrated—it creates severe internal friction. The observer goes to war with the biology, demanding a pristine, emotionless void that the Ape physically cannot provide.

### The Landing Pad Protocol
Because the Reality Engineer cannot stop the hardware from firing its ancient alarms, they stop trying. Instead of raising a defensive firewall, AdAM builds a "Landing Pad." When the Ape throws a spike of panic or annoyance, AdAM allows the emotion to land safely without judgment, acknowledges its evolutionary purpose, and lets it naturally phase-cancel into the ground. The alert is received, validated, and dismissed without taking the steering wheel.
