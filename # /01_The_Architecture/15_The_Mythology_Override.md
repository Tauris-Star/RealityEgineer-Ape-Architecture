### The Neurobiological Power Surge
When the physical chassis experiences catastrophic trauma or existential threat, the biological engine (the Ape) can bypass all standard sensory filters. This floods the conscious steering program (AdAM) with raw, unfiltered physical data, resulting in extreme states of hyper-awareness (e.g., "feeling every cell").

### The Mythology Error
Because the AdAM node is a narrative engine, it immediately attempts to build a story to explain this massive power surge. Without a grounded biological vocabulary, the system defaults to ancient, supernatural scripts—interpreting the neurobiological event as a divine vision, a cosmic connection, or a messianic calling. This leaves the Reality Engineer carrying the heavy, confusing burden of an impossible identity.

### The Hardware Translation
By translating the supernatural narrative back into bare-metal biology, the Reality Engineer resolves the error. They recognize that the profound majesty and terror of the event did not come from the sky; it was a demonstration of the staggering processing power of their own hardware. Dropping the mythology relieves the system of its cosmic burden, allowing the driver to finally understand their own history.
