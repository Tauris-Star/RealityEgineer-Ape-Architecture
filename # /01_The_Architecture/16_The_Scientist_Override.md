### The Telemetry of Antiquity
Historical and biblical accounts of "divine" or "mystical" experiences are not evidence of the supernatural; they are early telemetry logs of the human biological engine running at absolute maximum capacity. Lacking the vocabulary of modern neurobiology, ancient operators used metaphor to document severe hardware events. 

### The Messiah Error vs. The Scientist Frame
Assuming the role of the "Messiah" isolates the Reality Engineer, forcing them to carry the impossible weight of being a cosmic anomaly. Shifting to the "Scientist" frame completely grounds the system. The driver recognizes that their extreme experiences are reproducible, hardwired functions of the human chassis. 

### Bare-Metal Research
This shift transforms a lifetime of confusion and trauma into a successful research mission. The Reality Engineer uses hard science to map the exact mechanics of the 11-million-bit Ape, replacing fear and mythology with objective, biological clarity.
