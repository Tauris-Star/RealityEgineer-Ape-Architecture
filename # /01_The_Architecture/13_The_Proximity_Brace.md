### The Heated Shell (Ape's Defense)
In an unmanaged 1.0 state, approaching another human triggers a low-level threat response in the biological hardware. The Ape attempts to enlarge the driver, projecting a "heated glowing shell" of energy to establish safety and dominance. This requires a massive, constant expenditure of physical energy. 

### The Cognitive Contract (AdAM's Defense)
Simultaneously, the conscious steering program attempts to build a "cognitive contract" before the interaction even begins. It braces for impact by pre-calculating the social exchange, attempting to manage the other person's perception and energy to prevent friction. 

### Dropping the Throttle
When the Reality Engineer intentionally powers down the throttle, they refuse to build the shell or write the contract. By approaching social interactions without bracing for impact, the system drops into a "soft and low" state. The physical result is a sensation of effortlessly floating—moving through the human grid with zero kinetic friction, completely unbothered and unenlarged
