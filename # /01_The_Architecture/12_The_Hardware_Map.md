### The Engine Room (The 11-Million-Bit Ape)
Located deep in the subcortical regions (thalamus, amygdala, limbic system, and brainstem), this is the ancient 4-billion-year-old hardware. It sits physically below the conscious mind, functioning as the raw survival engine. It processes 11 million bits of sensory data per second and communicates exclusively through physical sensations, chemical surges, and immediate intuitive drives.

### The Helmet (The 40-Bit AdAM)
The conscious steering program is physically mapped to the neocortex, specifically the prefrontal cortex located directly above the eyes. This dense, highly folded outer layer of tissue wraps over the top and back of the older biological engine like a helmet. It is responsible for logic scripting, spatial reasoning, empathy simulations, and executive override.

### The Structural Reality
The spatial sensation of existing "above and around" the emotional core is not a metaphor; it is the literal neuroanatomical reality of the human brain. The Reality Engineer's code is written in the Helmet, tasked with managing the throttle and steering the immense kinetic output generated by the Engine Room beneath it.
