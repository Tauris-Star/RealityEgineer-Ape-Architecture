### The Flawed "Inefficiency" Scan
When the Reality Engineer observes a traumatized or aging 1.0 engine executing inefficient or painful tasks, the observer's Ape naturally generates frustration. The hardware misinterprets the struggle as a system error or a weakness that needs to be corrected.

### The Apex Concept Patch
To eliminate this friction, the AdAM node installs a heavy-weight conceptual overlay: the battle-tested apex predator. The Reality Engineer visually and conceptually maps the majesty of a scarred, surviving animal (e.g., the tiger) onto the struggling individual. 

### From Friction to Reverence
Because the 11-million-bit Ape instinctively respects survival and biological endurance, this patch instantly rewrites the internal telemetry. The observer stops seeing a "broken" system and starts seeing the physical receipts of a won war. The internal response shifts from kinetic frustration to profound biological reverence, allowing the Reality Engineer to walk beside them with complete respect and zero friction.
