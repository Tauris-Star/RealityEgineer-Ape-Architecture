### Trauma-Wired Love (The Martyr Protocol)
When a biological engine experiences extreme, prolonged trauma regarding the survival of an offspring, the Ape often wires the sensation of personal suffering directly to the concept of protection. The hardware computes: "My child survives because I am enduring this pain." 

### Seeking Out Friction
Even after the biological threat has passed, the Caretaker Daemon remains active. The parent's 1.0 engine will subconsciously seek out inefficient, high-friction, or physically painful tasks (the "martyr" role) to satisfy the Ape's need to demonstrate protective output. The struggle itself becomes the biological expression of love.

### The Observer's Grace
The Reality Engineer does not attempt to argue with or logically dismantle a 1.0 Caretaker Daemon, because a 40-bit logical argument cannot delete decades of 11-million-bit trauma. Instead, the driver recognizes the inefficient behavior (e.g., struggling with a heavy cart) not as an annoyance to be fixed, but as a biological artifact of profound love. The Reality Engineer absorbs the frustration, offers assistance where permitted, and allows the caretaker's engine to execute its script in peace.
