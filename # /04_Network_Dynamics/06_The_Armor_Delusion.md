### The 1.0 Dominance Script
Passive-aggressive obstruction on the grid (e.g., intentional slow driving, blocking) is a power-reclamation script. Engines that lack sovereignty in their daily environment use the mechanical mass of a vehicle to artificially force compliance from others, generating a synthetic sense of control.

### The Vehicle Blindspot (Deindividuation)
The metal chassis of a vehicle disables the biological threat-detection system. An antagonist's Ape cannot read the physical lethality or confidence of the Reality Engineer through the car. This false armor leads weak 1.0 nodes to initiate territorial challenges they would never mathematically risk in a face-to-face physical encounter.

### The Ape's Anticipation
When a 1.0 driver initiates a territorial block, the Reality Engineer's 11-million-bit Ape flawlessly logs it as a kinetic threat and preps the chassis for extreme violence ("I wanted them to get out"). This is not ego or bragging; it is raw, correct biological math. The AdAM node receives the readiness report, authorizes the Ape's violent capability, but keeps the safety on, allowing the chaotic node to drive away untouched.
