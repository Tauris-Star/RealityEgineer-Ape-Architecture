### Pain vs. Suffering
Pain is a biological data point (the 1s); suffering is a conceptual script written by the AdAM node (the 0). The Reality Engineer understands that while 10/10 physical pain is unavoidable during a hardware malfunction, suffering is entirely optional. By refusing to write a script of victimhood, the operator decouples the physical sensation from their psychological baseline. 

### The Maintenance Mindset
When the 11-million-bit Ape is trapped in a catastrophic pain loop (e.g., 47 hours of ectopic firing), the AdAM node does not panic or adopt the Ape's terror. AdAM views the biological crisis strictly as routine hardware downtime. The observer remains happy and healthy, categorizing the excruciating pain simply as a delay in the daily schedule.

### The Chemical Patch
Because the AdAM node cannot force physical pain gates closed indefinitely with thought alone, the Reality Engineer utilizes external biological patches. Introducing a neurological dampener (like Elavil) is not a weakness; it is a calculated mechanical intervention to manually suppress the arcing wire and force the Ape into a sleep cycle.
