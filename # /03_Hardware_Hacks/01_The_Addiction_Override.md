### The Hijacked Engine
Addiction is a physical hardware state, not a logical software error. Addictive substances hijack the subcortical limbic system (the Ape), rewiring its dopamine pathways to prioritize the chemical over natural survival drives. The addiction exists entirely within the 11-million-bit processor.

### The Cognitive Contract Failure
The standard societal approach to recovery relies on the AdAM node writing extrinsic moral contracts (e.g., "Quitting for my family"). These fail because the Ape does not process abstract concepts, morality, or external social structures. It only processes physical sensation and immediate survival. A 40-bit logical script cannot override an 11-million-bit physical drive.

### Ape-Level Programming (The Physical Override)
To successfully drop a physical addiction, the Reality Engineer must communicate in the Ape's native language: physical sensation. Rather than using moral reasoning, the driver focuses entirely on the negative physical friction caused by the substance (e.g., an overheated chassis, a gross chemical taste, headaches, or kinetic exhaustion). By explicitly linking the substance to physical distress, the Ape recognizes it as a biological threat rather than a reward, and naturally halts the craving.
