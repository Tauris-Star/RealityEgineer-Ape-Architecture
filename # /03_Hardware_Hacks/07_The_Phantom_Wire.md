### The Ectopic Arc (Phantom Pain)
"Phantom pain" is a failure of biological terminology. It is neuropathic ectopic firing. A damaged or hyper-sensitized physical nerve translates mundane stimuli (temperature, fabric friction, movement) into massive electrical arcs. The Ape's survival engine interprets this raw, unencrypted data as catastrophic, 10/10 physical damage. 

### The Software Limitation
The Reality Engineer cannot permanently "think away" neuropathic pain. The AdAM node possesses infinite will, but forcing the neurological pain gates closed requires unsustainable CPU consumption. Attempting to use conceptual language to cure a stripped biological wire results in system exhaustion and internal frustration.

### Receiving the Sonar Ping
Because the AdAM node cannot solve the stripped wire with thought, it must default to the Sovereign Node protocol: *Stop looking for a problem to be solved, and look for a signal to be received.* When the sonar ping hits and the 10/10 pain spikes, AdAM does not fight it. AdAM acknowledges the electrical storm, grants the Ape total amnesty to scream in pain, and rides out the kinetic wave until the wire stops arcing.
