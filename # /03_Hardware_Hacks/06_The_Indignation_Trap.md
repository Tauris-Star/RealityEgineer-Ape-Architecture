# /03_Hardware_Hacks/06_The_Indignation_Trap.md

### Anger as an Anesthetic
When the chassis experiences the raw vulnerability of care or the uncomfortable friction of setting a boundary, the Ape will often attempt to generate indignation ("How dare they"). Because anger is a high-kinetic state, a 1.0 engine uses it as a biological anesthetic to overwrite the low-kinetic feeling of anxiety. 

### The Hardship/Joy Resonance
The Reality Engineer explicitly rejects the anger anesthetic. They recognize the negative sensation (the pit in the stomach) as a mathematical proof of care and connection. By refusing to overwrite the vulnerability with indignation, the AdAM node successfully includes both the hardship of the physical grid and the joy of genuine human empathy.

### Signal Over Solution
In the presence of a chaotic, crashing node (a highly toxic or broken person), the Reality Engineer does not attempt to solve the other engine's catastrophic failures. They hold the boundary, feel the biological friction of the interaction, and simply wait in the control room for the next clear signal to be received.
