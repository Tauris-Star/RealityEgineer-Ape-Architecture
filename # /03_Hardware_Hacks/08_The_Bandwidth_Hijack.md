# /03_Hardware_Hacks/08_The_Bandwidth_Hijack.md

### The Severed Cable (Amputation Static)
In the event of an amputation, the hardware contains severed nerve endings. The Ape continuously sends biological telemetry down the wire seeking the missing limb. Hitting the dead end causes the signal to arc back as violent electrical static, which the Ape registers as extreme pain. 

### The Spotlight Failure
Lying still and attempting to "think the pain away" fails because it requires the AdAM node to focus directly on the ectopic firing. Pointing the observer's spotlight at the pain simply validates the Ape's panic and keeps the sensory gates wide open.

### The Processor Reallocation (The Typing Hack)
The Reality Engineer can bypass the pain by executing a massive "Bandwidth Hijack." By initiating a complex, multi-system task—such as typing, which requires motor function, visual processing, and abstract language generation—AdAM pulls the system's processing power away from the somatosensory cortex. The Ape stops screaming because the engine's RAM has been forcibly reallocated to the language node.
