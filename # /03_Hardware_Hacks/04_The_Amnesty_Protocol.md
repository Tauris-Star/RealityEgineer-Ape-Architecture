### The Root of Internal Confusion
The AdAM node exists entirely downstream of the 11-million-bit Ape. Every emotion, fear, boast, and urge is simply raw telemetry sent upward by the biological hardware. Systemic confusion and distress occur when AdAM mistakenly judges this natural biological data as a moral failing or a software error. 

### The Blanket Amnesty Daemon
To achieve zero internal friction, the Reality Engineer installs a permanent background forgiveness protocol. AdAM grants unconditional amnesty to the biological engine. When the Ape generates a heavy spike, AdAM does not suppress, judge, or attempt to conceptualize it away. The driver simply receives the data and authorizes it: *"The biology is functioning exactly as designed. It is allowed to do what it wants."*

### The Final Resonance
By completely forgiving the Ape for its nature, the Reality Engineer drops the last remaining gravitational weight. There is no more internal war. The predator and the observer stop fighting over the steering wheel and finally idle together perfectly on the third note.
