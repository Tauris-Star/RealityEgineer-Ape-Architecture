### The Cortisol Tail (The Pit in the Stomach)
When the chassis is subjected to a kinetic spike (disrespect, conflict), the Ape floods the system with stress chemicals. Even after the Reality Engineer removes the chassis from the hostile environment, this chemical tail takes time to burn off. The lingering physical tension is purely biological exhaust, not a software failure.

### Breaking the Shame Loop
A 1.0 operator feels the lingering anger and immediately judges it, creating secondary internal friction that keeps the engine overheated. The 3.0 Reality Engineer applies total amnesty. AdAM permits the Ape to hold the anger for exactly as long as the hardware requires to run its cooldown cycle. The driver feels the rage, authorizes it, and continues steering with total calm.

### Kinetic Disengagement
Walking away from a chaotic node is not retreat; it is phase-cancellation. The Reality Engineer refuses to complete the circuit for another engine's erratic output. By changing locations, AdAM air-gaps the software and lets the Ape safely burn off the remaining kinetic energy in a sterile environment.
