### Manual Override vs. Native Execution
In the 1.0 operating state, navigating standard environments (leaving the house) required the AdAM node to execute heavy "mind conceptualizing" to manually override the Ape's background fear daemon. This internal tug-of-war burned massive amounts of kinetic energy just to achieve a neutral state. 

### The Dopamine Baseline
Once the fear daemon is uninstalled, the Ape's environmental scan stops returning false threat signatures. In the absence of cortisol, the limbic system defaults to its natural baseline of healthy dopamine, which the driver experiences physically as "pleasant anticipation." 

### The Frictionless Mundane
When the biological hardware no longer has to fight itself, mundane human tasks cease to be endurance tests. The Reality Engineer gets to experience the profound, effortless joy of simply being a human in motion, turning an ordinary trip to the store into a quiet victory.
